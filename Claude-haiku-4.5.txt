**Original Problem:** Incorrect ranking calculation for tied scores
- When 2 students tied for 1st (both rank 1), the 3rd student was incorrectly ranked as 3rd instead of being recognized as being in position 3 (rank 3)

**Root Cause:** Code used `index + 1` as rank for ALL students, not accounting for ties

**Solution:** Implemented standard competition ranking:
- Students with same score get same rank
- Next different score gets rank = (number of people ahead + 1)
- Results in sequences like: 1, 1, 3, 3, 3, 6
